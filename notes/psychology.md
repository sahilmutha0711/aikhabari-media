## CURRENT TOOLKIT (use in every script)
1. Autopilot breaker: the first line must contradict a belief the viewer holds, or reveal a stake for THEM ("Everyone on H-1B thinks they get 60 days... A new rule wants to make it zero.").
2. Social currency: give one fact their friends don't know yet, or a ready-made opinion. People share what makes them look smart/funny/informed.
3. Re-commit beat within 3-6 s of the hook: a second reason to stay ("Watch till the end, number 3 is the one nobody's talking about" / "Save this, you'll need it on Nov 10").
4. Open loops every 8-10 s: numbered items ("rule two... rule three is the quiet one"), "and that's not even the worst part", a question you answer later.
5. Pattern interrupt every 2-3 s on screen (new visual, number pop, cut) and a tone change in the voice at the rehook.
6. Payoff last, end right after it; last line loops back to the hook word so replays feel natural.
7. Earn the send, don't demand it: make one line so useful that it names who needs it ("If you know someone on OPT, this is the date they need"). Instagram said it won't recommend content it believes explicitly asks for shares, comments or tags to "hack" reach; it called open-ended questions and CTAs fine. Use one open question ("what would you do?") over "comment YES" or a keyword. Like-asks stay rare, and only as the owner's call (max 2 engagement lines per Reel, playbook 00d).
8. Score each Reel on Mosseri's top three signals: average watch time, sends per reach, likes per reach. Sends matter slightly more for reaching non-followers, so judge growth Reels on sends per reach, not likes.

### 2026-11-05: Instagram's own ranking guidance and what it says about asks
Source quality: Mosseri and Instagram quotes below are as reported by Social Media Today, not read on Instagram directly. Instagram's own ranking explainer could not be opened in this run, and Meta's Transparency Center pages returned no body text.

**Findings**
1. Mosseri (Jan 2025): "The top three signals that matter most for ranking are watch time, likes and sends." He pointed creators to average watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach. "Likes are slightly more important for connected content, and sends are slightly more important for unconnected content." [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/)
   - Apply: for growth, the story needs a clear person who needs it. Saves are not in that top three, so playbook 00's "saves second" is our own priority, not Mosseri's stated ranking.
   - Lines (fill [ ] with verified facts only): "If you're on OPT, the number that matters here is [verified number]." / "Know someone on H-1B? This is the date they need: [verified date]."
2. Instagram (2024): "If we believe your content explicitly asks for engagement through shares, comments, tags, or other actions, then we won't recommend it." Its example was asking people to comment a specific word, number or emoji. [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-says-using-certain-ctas-can-impact-post-reach/717732/)
3. Instagram then clarified: "There haven't been changes to our recommendation guidelines." The test it gave is intent: it is engagement bait "if the end goal is to simply maximize the quantity of engagements as a way to 'hack' reach." "Open-ended questions and calls-to-action are a great way to engage with your audience." [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-clarifies-advice-single-word-ctas-longer-reels/718151/)
   - Limits: the original video was later deleted, the statements are from 2024, and nothing public shows how a spoken ask is detected. Treat this as a risk to manage, not a proven penalty.
   - Apply: this weakens the old toolkit claim that save/send asks are safe. Our "send this to..." triggers, "YES/NO" comment asks and "like this video" lines are all explicit asks, so they carry some of this risk. "Watch till the end" asks for no share, comment or tag, and it was in our best-held Reel (xAI).
   - Lines: "Would you take that deal? Tell me why." / "Who actually wins here? I want to hear your reasoning." / "I think [labelled take]. Change my mind."
4. Instagram's retention chart shows the percentage of views still watching at each moment; skip rate is reported as the share leaving in the first three seconds (secondary definition, not an Instagram quote). [Website Builder Expert](https://www.websitebuilderexpert.com/news/instagram-reel-retention-chart/)
   - Apply: use the chart to place open loops. Before scripting, note where the last Reel's curve dropped, then put the next reveal just ahead of that point.
   - Re-commit lines: "But that's the small number. The one that affects you is next." / "Here's where it gets strange."
