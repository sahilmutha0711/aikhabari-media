Before you shop this Black Friday: AI tools have been recommending fake shopping sites ⚠️

Scamwise checked 250,000+ shopping sites and flagged 100,000+ as scams. 74% used AI-made content, and a new one was reported about every 3 minutes.
NordVPN found 10,000+ copycat stores of brands like Adidas and Dr. Martens, and AI chatbots like Perplexity, ChatGPT and Gemini pointed people to some of them.

3 quick tips:
1. Type the brand's website yourself
2. Check the spelling of the link
3. Pay by credit card. A lock icon doesn't mean it's safe.
