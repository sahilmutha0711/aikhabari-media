Everyone thinks a layoff means walking out with nothing. Canva just did the opposite.

Canva is cutting 1,271 roles (about 22% of the company) after its growth slowed for the first time in a decade. Here's what everyone leaving gets:
• At least 6 months' salary, wherever they live
• Up to 12 months of healthcare (where Canva already offers it)
• They keep their company MacBook
• A $10,000 grant to start a business or project, with Canva taking 0% equity

Save this, and send it to your manager. This is the new bar.
